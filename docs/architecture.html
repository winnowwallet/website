Winnow · Architecture

How Winnow works.

Your phone finds and checks payments, keeps your wallet history, and signs with the keys you control.

Clearnet connections use your device’s IP address; your own Tor or I2P gateways can carry peer connections instead. Peers can see which blocks you request. Read the privacy and trust limits.
