02 / Direct to the network

Your phone.
Bitcoin.
Direct.

Your iPhone finds your transactions. You don’t need an extra computer or a company to look up your balance.

How does it stay so light?

Compact filters are small summaries of Bitcoin blocks. Your phone checks them locally and downloads blocks that may contain your transactions. Some matches are false alarms, so it can download more than it needs. This uses more data than an address-lookup service, while avoiding sending it a list of your addresses.

It is still a light client: it relies on network peers and does not validate the whole chain like a full node. Bitcoin transactions are public; your IP address and requested blocks can reveal information.

03 / Phone recovery

New phone.
Same wallet.

Restore from your encrypted backup. Pick up where you left off.

What gets backed up?

Winnow encrypts your phone key and saved wallet state before storing them in iCloud. The decryption key syncs through end-to-end encrypted iCloud Keychain. Recovery needs a successful backup and access to the required Apple Account and Keychain. This does not replace a missing hardware key.

07 / Recovery + family

A plan for you.
And your
family.

Choose who can help you recover access—and who can help your family when you’re gone.

Agree instructions with your trustee, then set your signing and recovery rules.

An optional recovery delay runs from each coin’s confirmation, not from a loss or death.

Who can transfer funds without me?

In the preceding any-2-of-3 setup, your trustee and provider can transfer funds together without your signature or a waiting period. This is part of the normal spending rule. The optional recovery delay adds another route; it does not restrict that normal route.

The recovery keys and number of approvals depend on the policy you create. Bitcoin measures delays in blocks, so days are approximate. See the approval rules ↗

A few useful details.

Do I need a desktop or my own server?

No. Winnow connects directly to Bitcoin peers from your iPhone. You need an internet connection; iCloud recovery has separate Apple Account and Keychain requirements.

Do I need a hardware wallet?

No. You can begin with Winnow on your phone. The two-device protection described here applies to a configured 2-of-2 policy using supported hardware.

Can my trustee and provider send without me?

Yes, together, in the any-2-of-3 example shown here. Your approval is not required when those two approve. A separate recovery delay does not change that normal spending rule. Understand the approval rules ↗
