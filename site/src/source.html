The founder

Alex Newman.
Security, systems
and open source.

Alex built Winnow around a practical ambition: make Bitcoin useful and approachable on an iPhone, with protection that can grow as people’s needs change.

Alex co-founded hCaptcha / Intuition Machines and Ohmdata, the distributed database company acquired by WANdisco. His security work includes co-authoring CLASP and multiple wins in DEFCON’s Capture the Flag. He has contributed to Linux and FreeBSD and worked as an Apache committer.

Alex also founded HUMAN Protocol, where his protocol design work connects human judgment with on-chain systems.

With Winnow, that experience is being applied to the choices around holding Bitcoin: direct network access, independent signing keys and recovery—and, in its next chapter, trustees and compatible services. The aim is to make those choices understandable and manageable from one phone.

The engineering is open to inspection. Alex built an independent implementation of Bitcoin signing and has rewritten the wallet in Lean, where specified properties can be checked with proofs. Source code, engineering decisions and recorded tests sit alongside the product.
