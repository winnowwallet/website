November 2026 · Lean wallet

A new foundation.
A familiar wallet.

Winnow’s wallet and native interface now run in Lean. A unified home brings your balance, activity and protection choices together.

  • One home, with network, security and backup controls in Settings.
  • Per-person payment cards without sharing your wallet’s public key.
  • A single secp256k1 implementation for keys, signatures and checks.
  • Join existing native SegWit multisig accounts and sign their externally prepared payments.
